SandBoxed

SandBoxed is a small shared 2D world. You can host a new sandbox or join one.

In the sandbox you make your own objects. You write what you want, for example "a red ball that jumps". The AI on your own computer turns your words into a small script.

Your object is sent to the other players. Their computers check it automatically. If it follows the rules and is safe, it is accepted and comes to life in the sandbox. If not, it is rejected.

The game runs between the players. If the host leaves, the next best player takes over.

You need an AI to play: the AI built into Chrome, a model in your browser, or your own AI server like Ollama or LM Studio.
